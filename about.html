About Johannesburg SPCA

The Johannesburg SPCA is an animal welfare organisation dedicated to protecting animals and promoting responsible animal care within the community.

The organisation works to prevent animal cruelty and provides care and support for animals in need.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote animal welfare, prevent cruelty and encourage responsible pet ownership.

How We Help

  • Animal welfare and protection
  • Animal adoption
  • Community education
  • Support for animals in need
